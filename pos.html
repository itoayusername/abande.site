Quick Plan & Billing Notes

Partner vs. Premium

Partner has a ₱0 base monthly fee with 0.008% accrued per non-voided completed sale and includes full Premium capabilities. Premium charges a fixed ₱149 / month with no per-transaction share.

Online Store Add-On (+₱300 / mo)

Optional on any plan. Syncs incoming online orders & reservations into POS Orders, Tasks, and Reservations, displays live stock & featured listings, and routes workflows to matching business & staff.

Chat Retention & Team Scale

Free retains basic text chat for 1 day (24 hours) with up to 2 staff accounts. Partner and Premium extend chat retention up to 15 days and support up to 15 staff accounts.
