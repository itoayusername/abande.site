Before you scan

  • Light: bright and even. Face a window or lamp; don’t sit with a light behind you.
  • Background: plain and dark if possible. Keep white walls and shirts out of the cube’s area.
  • Distance: hold it 20–30 cm away so it fills about a third of the frame.
  • Hands: hold by the edges; keep fingers off the stickers.
  • Glare: tilt slightly if a sticker looks shiny or washed out.
  • Steps: scan 6 flat sides, then play. Corner views (3 sides showing) are best while turning.

Floating Cube

Hold a real, standard-colour Rubik’s cube (white opposite yellow) in front of your camera, in good light. The game scans all 6 sides, checks it understood your cube, then the floating cube follows your hand. During the scramble it checks that you really turned each side before moving on. The camera is required. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
