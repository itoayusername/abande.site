Reels Studio

Write the script, add image prompts, and get a finished vertical reel with voiceover and captions.

Settings

Script

Paste the whole script. Each paragraph becomes a scene. To set an image yourself, add a line starting with Image: inside the paragraph. A script with no blank lines is split by sentence.

Voiceover

Single take reads the whole script in one go, so tone, pace and volume stay the same, then splits the scenes at the natural pauses. Separate clips are quicker to redo. Or upload your own recording.

Scenes

Each scene has an image and a duration (seconds on screen). Edit anything, then redo just that part.
