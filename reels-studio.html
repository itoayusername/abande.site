Reels Studio

Write the script, add image prompts, and get a finished vertical reel with voiceover and captions.

Settings

Script

Paste your whole script. Blocks labeled Script: and Image Prompt: are sorted into the right fields automatically, with or without markdown, quotes or “Prompt 1” headings. Plain paragraphs also work: each one becomes a scene. You can also put the image prompts in the second box, one per scene, in the same order.

Voiceover

Single take reads the whole script in one go, so tone, pace and volume stay the same, then splits the scenes at the natural pauses. Separate clips are quicker to redo. Or upload your own recording.

Waveform of your voiceover. Numbers mark where each image starts. Drag a line to change when an image switches, or click to jump there in the preview.

Scenes

Each scene has an image and a duration (seconds on screen). Edit anything, then redo just that part.

Make the image in your Gemini tab, copy it (right-click the image, Copy image), then press Paste image. You can also drop an image file here.
